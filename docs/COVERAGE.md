## Scope and review

This is a curated, expanding introduction rather than a complete world register or a ranking. Research notes document remaining gaps and proposed candidates. Coverage still depends on available sources. Categories are modern navigation aids, not claims that historical people used those labels themselves.

Source consultation, bilingual coverage and automated validation are recorded separately from independent historical and native-language review, which remain pending. The category research logs are in [research](../research/README.md); specific sources and consultation dates are attached to each public record.

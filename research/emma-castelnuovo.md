# Emma Castelnuovo — addition, 27 November 2026

Added `emma-castelnuovo` with mathematics and women-in-science memberships. The record covers her mathematics education work, including active geometry teaching and books; no portrait was added.

Sources consulted:

- [MacTutor biography](https://mathshistory.st-andrews.ac.uk/Biographies/Castelnuovo_Emma/): life dates 1913–2014, classroom methods, exhibitions and bibliography for the 1946 paper and the 1976 book with Mario Barra.
- [ICMI Newsletter 27, July 2014](https://www.mathunion.org/fileadmin/ICMI/News/2014/Newsletter27.pdf), page 2: obituary documenting persecution, teaching and educational approach.
- [Treccani biography by Carla Degli Esposti and Nicoletta Lanciano](https://www.treccani.it/enciclopedia/emma-castelnuovo_(Dizionario-Biografico)/): career and publication chronology.

MacTutor dates Geometria intuitiva to 1948; the detailed Treccani account distinguishes its writing in 1948 from publication in 1949. The work’s date therefore remains a 1948–1949 range, with a bilingual explanation. This uncertainty concerns the book, not her life dates. Independent historical and language review remains pending.

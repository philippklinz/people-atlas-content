# Political figures: expansion research log

Research and authoring date: 2026-11-27. File: `people/expansion-politicians.json`.

## Scope and status

This batch adds 40 deceased political figures, including 12 women, across ancient, medieval and modern periods. It is an initial expansion, not a complete or representative account of world political history. Monarchs, heads of government, revolutionary organizers and suffrage campaigners share the existing `politicians` category. Inclusion records historical significance and does not confer approval. No portraits or inferred identity fields were added.

Each record has original German and English summaries, a short biography and dated political events. Institutional pages, archival catalogues and scholarly material listed below were consulted through retrieved page text or indexed passages. Where a scholarly publisher exposed only an abstract or extract, that was the material read; full books were not reviewed. Both languages together remain below 200 prose words per record, before this short research note. No quotations are reproduced.

Historical subject-specialist review and independent bilingual editorial review remain pending. The notes identify specific gaps for a longer biography pass. Government, party and commemorative sources are used with attention to their standpoint; their praise, priority claims and unsupported superlatives are not adopted.

## Validation

- All 40 records pass `schemas/1.0.0/person.schema.json` under Draft 2020-12 with URI/date format checking.
- German biographies contain 44–52 whitespace-delimited words; English biographies contain 46–56 words.
- Every record has birth/death data, both languages, at least one dated event and source URLs. All statuses are `deceased`; no date uses year zero.
- Individual life/event order and global person-ID duplication are checked separately from schema validity. Final duplicate status is recorded in the handoff; other agents may still be editing.
- Catalog entry, checksums, release metadata and integrated repository checks are intentionally left to the coordinating agent.

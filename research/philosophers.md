## Scope and review status

Each profile has original German and English text, a short biography, one or two contributions, and consulted sources. No portraits were added.

Source consultation and schema validation are complete for this collection. Independent historical review and bilingual editorial review remain pending; these profiles are not marked as reviewed. No catalog or translation-status file was edited by this category agent.

## Modeling and coverage decisions

Unknown life endpoints were retained for Laozi and Nāgārjuna rather than converting a traditional narrative or an activity period into a continuous lifespan. Xunzi has no assigned death year. The current data model has no separate activity-interval field, which could help represent such people more usefully in a future timeline.

Women with evidenced mathematical or natural-science work receive both their subject category and `women-in-science`: Elisabeth of Bohemia and Hildegard of Bingen. The additional category does not replace their other categories. A philosophy profile alone does not establish a natural-science contribution.

Marx, Beauvoir and Hildegard were coordinated with the politics, writing and natural-science agents respectively. Ibn Sina remains with natural sciences. Kant and Wollstonecraft already exist and were not duplicated.

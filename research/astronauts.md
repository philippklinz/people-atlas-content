# Astronauts — 28 November 2026

This collection follows 27 people who flew in space, from Vostok 1 in 1961 to the Ignis mission in 2025. The category includes cosmonauts, taikonauts, shuttle and station crews, and a privately funded participant. Each record describes what the person did on a particular flight. The three Apollo 11 profiles distinguish the work aboard Eagle from Michael Collins’s work aboard Columbia in lunar orbit.

## Sources and editorial decisions

- National space agencies and participating research institutions document the missions. Individual profiles also draw on first-person accounts, including Phạm Tuân’s interview with the Vietnamese government newspaper and Helen Sharman’s account of experiments on Mir for Imperial College London.
- Mission details can connect people across decades. Mae Jemison was a co-investigator on the STS-47 bone-cell experiment that Peggy Whitson helped develop before her own spaceflights. Their profiles cite NASA’s records for both roles.
- For Austromir, the profile gives the launch and return dates, 2–10 October 1991. The duration of the whole flight and the time aboard Mir are different intervals. Austria in Space documents how the Motomir ergometer measured muscle performance as well as providing exercise.
- Shenzhou 9 visited the Tiangong 1 laboratory in 2012. Liu Yang’s 2022 spacewalk took place at the Wentian module of the later Tiangong station. Her profile identifies the destination on each flight.
- Mark Shuttleworth’s record identifies his self-funded role and the university experiments he performed on the ISS. Additional category memberships follow the work described in each record, including Alexei Leonov’s painting, Sally Ride’s physics and science writing, and Harrison Schmitt’s geology and Senate service.

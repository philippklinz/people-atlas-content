# Grete Hermann — addition, 27 November 2026

Added `grete-hermann` to the women-in-science collection, retaining mathematics, physics and philosophy memberships. Alternate publication names are recorded as aliases.

Evidence consulted:

- [Reichenberger, Physics Today (2024)](https://physicstoday.aip.org/features/grete-hermanns-ethical-philosophy-of-physics): life dates 1901–1984 and the 1950 Bremen professorship; cautions against overstating the von Neumann critique.
- [Bacciagaluppi, Physics and Beyond (2020)](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s10838-020-09529-z): intellectual biography, finite algebraic procedures, 1935 essay, exile and educational work.
- [Original article metadata, Mathematische Annalen](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/BF01206635): publication in volume 95 (1926), pages 736–788. Full text was not consulted through the subscription preview.

The doctorate (1925), journal publication (1926) and later electronic publication date (2016) are distinct. The record criticizes the scope of von Neumann’s argument rather than claiming a simple refutation of quantum theory. No portrait was added.

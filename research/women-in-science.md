## Source cautions

- Agnesi’s Bologna appointment is not presented as evidence of actual classroom teaching.
- Lepaute’s almanac coverage years are not treated as publication dates.
- Sohonie: Institute of Science gives 1911; the Indian Academy of Sciences account gives 1912. The range remains unresolved. Broad claims that she was India’s first female scientist are omitted.
- Tsujimura: the university page is inconsistent about the date and form of isolated tannin. The record uses catechin instead.
- Ball: UH dates the method to 1916; the 1915 master’s degree is not used to date the method. No cure claim or questionable solubility explanation is repeated.
- Chowdhuri’s record uses a dated publication rather than resolving inconsistent accounts of her doctoral award year. The 1941 Nature paper is reproduced in the institutional Physics News PDF.
- Elion: the accessible NAS memoir and its bibliography support the dated purine paper. Conflicting shorthand discovery dates in popular accounts are not used.
- Levi-Montalcini’s 1954 publication metadata was available in the indexed journal record; direct PMC opening encountered a CAPTCHA. The record describes the joint paper, without choosing a single discovery year for a multistage research programme.

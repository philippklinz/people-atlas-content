# Mathematics expansion research log

Review date: 27 November 2026. Collection: `people/expansion-mathematicians.json`.

This first expansion adds 32 people, including eight women who retain `mathematicians` and also belong to `women-in-science`. It broadens the chronology and geography but does not claim completeness or representative coverage. English and German prose is original and AI-assisted. Independent historical and native-language review is pending.

## Scope and unresolved review

- The current evidence base relies heavily on MacTutor. More independent specialist and local-language scholarship should be added during deeper biography review. Ancient records distinguish approximate chronology from firm dates. An unknown contribution date is retained when a date could not be supported from the consulted text.
- Pythagoras is presented with attribution uncertainty; later school traditions are not treated as proof of his personal discoveries. Fermat’s birth range records the disagreement between the cited biography and archival discussion.
- Source inconsistencies involving Kovalevskaya’s prize, Germain’s competition, Robinson’s presidency and Browne’s doctorate chronology or priority were avoided instead of silently choosing uncertain details. These remain topics for specialist review.
- Category membership is an editorial navigation aid. Astronomy is grouped under the existing physics category; historical disciplinary boundaries were different.
- The full dataset and engine rendering are integrated by the parent task. Passing structural checks is not historical certification.
